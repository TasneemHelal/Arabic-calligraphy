## Step 1: Reference analysis

| # | Reference | Lettering | Composition | Color | Mood |
|---|-----------|-----------|-------------|-------|------|
| R1 | Burgundy, interwoven ribbons on white | Free modern Jali-Diwani / *calligraffiti*. | R2 | Crimson, two columns, angular | Contemporary flat-brush hand lettering. | R3 | Red-to-maroon gradient on parchment | Dense, free Diwani-style word block. Letters sit on rising diagonal baselines, and dots and small diacritics fill the counters. | Tall rectangle; the text is the texture. | **Vertical gradient**: bright red at the top to dark maroon at the bottom. Warm parchment ground. | Romantic, classical, warm |
| R4 | Vermilion on cream with a seal | Same family as R3, with lighter strokes and more air between words. | Rectangle with a small artist seal centered underneath. | Flat vermilion `≈#D3202A` on cream. | Fresh, gallery-like |

### Visual DNA (what the collection should share)
1. **Text as texture.** Words lock together into one rectangular mass, and the edges of the block stay mostly straight.
2. **Vertical anchors.** Alif, lam and *kaf* uprights set the rhythm. Curves (*ya'*, *nun*, *ra'*, *waw*) sweep across between them.
3. **Monochrome red family.** One ink in two or three values, sometimes with a gradient. No second hue.
4. **Fillers are real marks.** Dots, *fatha*/*kasra*/*shadda* and small letters fill the counters. They are never random scribbles.
5. **Generous outer margin** around a dense center, the way a gallery print is matted.
6. **Readable on a second look.** The art draws you in first, then the words become legible.

## Step 4: Creative directions & palettes

All colors are specified in sRGB HEX. For print, files go out in CMYK (FOGRA39 / Coated) with a soft-proof check.

### A. Oxblood Ribbons (from R1)
- **Phrase fit:** 4, 5, 3
- **Primary** Wine `#8A1538` · **Secondary** Oxblood `#4E0B1D` (back layer) · **Accent** Rosewood `#B23A5A` (the 1–2 hero strokes only)
- **Ink gradient** (on hero strokes only): `#A21E48` 0% → `#8A1538` 55% → `#5C0E26` 100%, at 35° along the stroke direction
- **Background:** Porcelain `#FBF8F4`, flat, with no texture. The crisp edge is part of the look.
- **Finish:** matte giclée on cotton rag. Optional raised gloss varnish on the hero strokes.

### B. Ember Columns (from R2)
- **Phrase fit:** 2, 7 (as a diptych), 8
- **Primary** Crimson `#8E1116` · **Secondary** Ember `#5E0A0D` (shadow notch on the strokes) · **Accent** none, so the look stays strictly graphic
- **Gradient:** none. Flat ink only.
- **Background:** Bone `#F3F0EA` with an optional ghost botanical line-drawing in `#DCD8D1` at 30% opacity, kept 100% outside the text block.
- **Finish:** matte poster, or uncoated 300 gsm.

### C. Sunset Ink (from R3)
- **Phrase fit:** 1, 10, 3
- **Ink gradient**, vertical, top to bottom: `#E4262F` 0% → `#C8192B` 35% → `#9A1228` 65% → `#5A0C1A` 100%
- **Background:** Parchment `#EFE5D3` with fine paper grain (monochrome noise, 3–4% opacity) and a very soft vignette to `#E6D9C2` at the corners
- **Accent:** artist seal in Ink Black `#2A2422`
- **Finish:** fine-art textured paper (e.g. Hahnemühle William Turner).

### D. Vermilion Seal (from R4)
- **Phrase fit:** 9, 6, 5
- **Primary** Vermilion `#D3202A` · **Secondary** Cinnabar `#B0161F` (diacritics and dots only, so they read clearly) · **Accent** seal `#2A2422`
- **Gradient:** none
- **Background:** Cream `#F6F1E6`, flat, or with a 2% grain
- **Finish:** matte giclée. The most "gallery" piece in the set.

### E. Midnight Gilt (new, extends the collection)
- **Phrase fit:** 1, 7, 2
- **Background:** Midnight `#0F1A2B`, with an optional subtle radial lift to `#16253B` in the center
- **Ink:** metallic gold gradient `#F1D592` 0% → `#D4A24C` 40% → `#A97A2C` 75% → `#7A5520` 100%, plus 2–3 thin highlight strokes in `#FFF1C9`
- **Finish:** real gold foil (hot-foil or Metallic Inkjet) is recommended. A printed "gold" gradient looks flat, so the PNG preview is only a simulation.

### F. Terracotta & Sage (new, interior-trend)
- **Phrase fit:** 5, 8, 6
- **Primary** Terracotta `#B4543A` → `#7A2E1F` (vertical gradient) · **Accent** Sage `#8B9A7C` (small dots and diacritics only) · **Background** Linen `#EEE9DF`
- **Finish:** matte, uncoated.

### Collection cohesion rules
- Every piece uses one ink family plus at most one accent.
- Margins are fixed relative to the short side: **8% outer safe margin** for the text block, plus a **3 mm bleed** on every printed edge.
- Same artist-seal position (centered beneath, 4% of height) on every piece in the set.

## Production approach (honest capability note)

The references are **hand-drawn** compositions. To guarantee correct Arabic (proper joining, dots, ligatures, diacritics), production will use real OpenType Arabic shaping (HarfBuzz) with open-licensed calligraphic fonts. Candidates are Aref Ruqaa (Ruqʿah), Amiri (Naskh), Reem Kufi, Alkalami and Gulzar. Each word is then:

1. shaped as a real Arabic word, so letters are never assembled by hand;
2. converted to vector outlines;
3. arranged into the composition by scale, rotation, layering, stretched-*kashida* lengthening and overlap rules;
4. colored with the gradients above;
5. exported as **SVG (true vector)**, a **PDF** built from the same vector and sized with bleed, and a **PNG at 300 DPI**.

**Limitation:** this produces correct, elegant, composed calligraphic typography. It cannot produce the free, hand-improvised letter distortions of a master *khattat* (the extreme ribbon stretches in R1 are an example). For those, the generated SVG is the right base for a calligrapher, or for you in Illustrator, to refine by hand. Nothing is called vector or print-ready unless the file actually is.
