# Sample 08: الحبّ · "Interwoven" (dense rebuild of sample 07 rev 10)

The client asked for the density and interlocking of a hand-made reference. This rebuild of sample 07 (which stays unchanged) makes these changes:

| Change | Setting |
|---|---|
| Heavier interlacing | Thin strokes may cross up to 22 % of a word's ink (sample 07: 12 %). Letter bodies, loops and dots stay protected |
| Long extensions | Main words may extend horizontally up to 1.5× |
| Mixed scales | 50 large words (70 mm em), then 67 smaller woven-in words at 72 %, 56 % and 44 % scale, all from the same 16 words |
| Tight packing | 0.8 mm clearance (was 1.6 mm). Ink coverage 40 % (sample 07: about 30 %) |
| Integrated marks | 450 harakat, each within 8 mm of a word and at least 5.5 mm apart |
| Kept | Client's 16 words, red→maroon gradient, cream paper, 3 in padding, A1 at 300 DPI |

Limitation: Gulzar has no tatweel glyph, so "extension" is a horizontal widening of the whole word, not a drawn kashida stroke. Hand-drawn letter reshaping of the reference's kind cannot be generated from a font.

Regenerate: `python3 designs/love_interwoven.py`
